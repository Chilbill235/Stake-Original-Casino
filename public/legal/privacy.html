Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Stake Originals ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our social casino and sweepstakes gaming service (the "Service"). By creating an account or otherwise using the Service, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

To operate the Service and comply with sweepstakes and financial regulations, we collect the following categories of information:

  • Account Information — username, email address, password (hashed using bcrypt), date of birth, state/jurisdiction of residence.
  • Identity Verification (KYC) — government-issued ID, selfie verification, and proof of address when you redeem Sweeps Coins for cash prizes. KYC data is processed by a regulated third-party verification provider.
  • Transaction Data — purchase history, Sweeps Coin redemptions, deposit and withdrawal amounts, payment-method metadata, and crypto wallet addresses you choose to share.
  • Gameplay Telemetry — bets placed, multipliers hit, streaks, and bonus activity. This data powers our rewards system (Daily Bonus, Challenges, Rakeback, Affiliate).
  • Device & Technical Data — IP address, browser type, device identifiers, and session cookies for fraud prevention and platform integrity.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information above to:

  • Operate the Service, authenticate users, and process payments and redemptions.
  • Comply with sweepstakes laws, anti-money-laundering (AML) obligations, and geolocation requirements (we do not serve users in excluded jurisdictions).
  • Detect and prevent fraud, bonus abuse, multi-accounting, and other prohibited activity.
  • Power our rewards systems — Daily Bonus, Challenges, Rakeback, and Affiliate commissions — and award Sweeps Coins to you.
  • Send transactional notifications (security alerts, redemption status). We do not sell your data to third parties for marketing.

3. Sweeps Coins & Promotional Use

Sweeps Coins (SC) are promotional credits redeemable 1-for-1 for cash prizes. SC has no cash value until redeemed. You never need to purchase SC — SC is granted as a free bonus with Gold Coin purchases, daily login, and other promotions. See our Sweepstakes Rules for full details.

4. Cookies

We use strictly necessary cookies to keep you signed in and remember your preferences (currency selection, sound effects). We do not use third-party advertising cookies. You can disable cookies in your browser, but doing so will break authentication.

5. Data Sharing

We share information only with:

  • Payment processors (Stripe, crypto on-chain networks) to process purchases and redemptions.

  • Regulators and law enforcement when required by law.
  • Affiliates — when you sign up using a referral link, the referring user sees your username and signup date so they can track their referral performance. No contact information is shared.

We never sell your personal information to data brokers.

6. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active, plus a minimum of 5 years after account closure to comply with financial and anti-fraud regulations. KYC records are retained for the period required by applicable law.
