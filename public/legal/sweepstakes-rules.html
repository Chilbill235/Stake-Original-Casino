4. Playthrough Requirement

Before SC may be redeemed for a cash prize, it must be wagered at least one time (1x) on any game. SC is divided into two balances:

  • Unplayed SC — eligible to wager but not to redeem.
  • Redeemable SC — has met the 1x wagering requirement and is eligible for redemption.

When you wager SC, the wagered amount is moved from Unplayed to Redeemable. Winnings are paid in Redeemable SC.

5. Prize Redemption

Once your Redeemable SC balance is at least 50.00 SC and your account is fully verified (KYC approved), you may request redemption. Redemptions are processed within 1–5 business days. Prizes are paid in U.S. dollars via:

  • Bank transfer (ACH)

The maximum prize value per redemption is $10,000 USD. Multiple redemptions per week are permitted within this cap.

6. Verification (KYC)

Before your first redemption you must complete identity verification. This requires:

  • A government-issued photo ID (driver's license or passport).
  • A selfie photo matching the ID.
  • Proof of address (utility bill or bank statement, less than 90 days old).

Verification is performed by a regulated third-party provider and typically completes within 24 hours.

7. Game Rules & Provably Fair

Every game round uses a cryptographic commit-reveal scheme:

  • Before play, the server seed hash is displayed.
  • The client seed is rotated by the user.
  • After each round the unhashed seeds are revealed, letting you independently verify the outcome.

You can audit any past round by pasting the seeds into our Provably Fair verifier.
